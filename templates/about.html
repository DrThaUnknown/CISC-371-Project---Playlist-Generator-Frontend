About Us

PlayGen was created to make music discovery faster and more personal. Instead of searching through playlists or guessing what to play, you simply tell us your activity like studying, gym, relaxing, gaming, and we instantly generate songs that match your vibe.

This project was built using Python, Flask, HTML, CSS, and a curated music recommendation system powered by Last.fm. Our mission is to create a simple and fun way for anyone to find the right soundtrack for any moment.

More features coming soon: mood detection, deeper Last.fm integration, and personal playlist saving.
